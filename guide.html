1. Lead → CRM → Slack alert

Webhook catches a form submission, writes a row to your sheet/CRM, and pings Slack. Trigger: webhook → actions: sheet, slack.

2. Scheduled API pull → Email digest

Cron every morning fetches your key metric and emails a one-line digest. Trigger: schedule → action: email.

3. New email → Notion + Telegram

Watch an inbox, save important threads to Notion, mirror to Telegram. Trigger: email → actions: notion, telegram.

4. New row → CRM API + Slack

A new signup row triggers an HTTP call to your CRM and a team notification. Trigger: new row → actions: crm, slack.

5. Validate before you ship

Export your n8n workflow and run validate.py to catch broken connections first. Free tool: para-inc-tools.
